# G2Gcov 0.1.0

* Initial CRAN release.
* `G2G_static_MLE()`: maximum likelihood estimation for G2G models with time-invariant covariates.
* `G2G_varying_MLE()`: maximum likelihood estimation for G2G models with time-varying covariates in person-period format.
* `plot_p0()`: visualize the implied gamma distribution over baseline churn propensity.
* `plot_survival_insample_g2g()`: in-sample G2G survivor curve.
* `plot_survival_holdout_g2g()`: hold-out G2G survivor curve fitted on training data.
* `plot_insample_holdout_paper()`: paper-style actual-vs-model survival and retention plots with optional group faceting.
* Includes `kb_data`, a sample person-period dataset used in the package vignettes.
